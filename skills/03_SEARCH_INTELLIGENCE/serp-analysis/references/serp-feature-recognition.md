## Operator notes
Capture the query exactly as tested, plus locale, device context when available, date, major result types, organic formats, and notable feature modules. Read representative results and record what information is actually being surfaced. Treat the SERP as a current observation that may change, not as a permanent specification. ## Stop conditions
