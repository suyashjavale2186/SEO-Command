# Competitor Comparability

This reference is operational rather than adversarial: it tells the **competitor-analysis** skill how to construct a clean comparison set that can support SERP, content, and authority analysis. [Evidence Tier: Tier 3 — Industry observation]

## Build the comparison set
Classify candidates by role before comparing them. A content competitor competes for the reader's task; a commercial competitor competes for the buyer; an authority source may dominate because of institutional evidence. Keep these labels separate in the dataset so the analyst does not mix unlike pages. [Evidence Tier: Tier 3 — Industry observation]

For each candidate, record query overlap, audience, geography, page type, business model, and evidence strength. A candidate becomes stronger when several dimensions align. A candidate with only keyword overlap should be retained as an observation, not treated as a complete benchmark. [Evidence Tier: Tier 3 — Industry observation]

## Concrete scenario
On “how to choose payroll software,” a product vendor and a software-review site are both prominent. The analyst compares their page structures only after establishing that both address the buyer's selection task. A government payroll guide is kept separately as a factual source because its page role and incentive structure differ. [Evidence Tier: Tier 3 — Industry observation]

## How the comparison is used
Use comparable competitors to inspect content coverage, evidence, format, internal linking, and authority context. Use unlike competitors to understand search-result diversity or factual expectations, not to infer a universal content template. [Evidence Tier: Tier 3 — Industry observation]

## Failure modes
Confusing domain authority with page comparability, mixing local and global markets, treating a news result as a product comparator, and selecting the benchmark after seeing the desired conclusion. [Evidence Tier: Tier 3 — Industry observation]

## Evidence boundary
Competitor analysis can observe relationships and outcomes but cannot establish a private Google mechanism. Unverified causal stories are **Tier 4 — Hypothesis**. [Evidence Tier: Tier 1 — Officially documented]
