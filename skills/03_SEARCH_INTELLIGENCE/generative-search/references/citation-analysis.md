- # Technique 06: GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) [Evidence Tier: Tier 3 — Industry observation]


- ## What It Is [Evidence Tier: Tier 3 — Industry observation]


- Generative-search optimization should focus on answer clarity, unique evidence, entity clarity, crawlability, and trustworthy sourcing rather than secret tricks. Academic and industry studies can inform hypotheses, but do not establish a proprietary ranking formula. [Evidence Tier: Tier 2 — Strong empirical evidence]


- ## Why It Works [Evidence Tier: Tier 3 — Industry observation]


- AI systems cite content that is: easy to extract, verifiable, authoritative, and structured for natural language queries. Content optimized for AI citation also tends to rank well in traditional search because the same qualities (clarity, specificity, authority) that AI systems value are what Google's quality signals reward. [Evidence Tier: Tier 3 — Industry observation]


- ## Key Statistics [Evidence Tier: Tier 3 — Industry observation]


- - Third-party studies indicate that AI Overview citations are not perfectly limited to the highest-ranking traditional pages, so evaluate generative visibility separately from classic rankings. [Evidence Tier: Tier 3 — Industry observation]

## Triage method
Build a query set that represents the business’s important questions, then record whether the site is cited, mentioned, or associated with entities in observed AI-search answers. Save the source context and date. Repeat observations before making a pattern claim and note that generated answers can vary. [Evidence Tier: Tier 1 — Officially documented]

## Scenario walk-through
A SaaS brand monitors queries around its core compliance category and sees it cited in some answers but only mentioned in others. The measurement log separates those states and records the cited passage, destination URL, and competing sources rather than rolling them into one “AI score.” [Evidence Tier: Tier 3 — Industry observation]

## Escalation triggers
Treating mentions as citations; measuring only a single query; assuming one model’s response represents all generative search; or claiming special schema/phrase tricks guarantee inclusion. [Evidence Tier: Tier 1 — Officially documented]
