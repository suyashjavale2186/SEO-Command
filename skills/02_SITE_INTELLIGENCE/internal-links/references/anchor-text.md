- # Anchor Text Safety Guide [Evidence Tier: Tier 3 — Industry observation]


- Anchor text is the clickable text in a backlink. Google reads it as a strong relevance signal, which is exactly why it's the easiest thing to over-optimize and the first thing Google's spam systems look at. This guide is about avoiding two specific failure modes: the Penguin-era exact-match penalties that still apply today, and the modern SpamBrain classification that treats anchor text patterns as a primary spam signal. [Evidence Tier: Tier 3 — Industry observation]


- ## A short history [Evidence Tier: Tier 3 — Industry observation]


- Historical lesson: Google has long published and enforced policies against manipulative linking. The practical takeaway is to avoid manufactured anchor patterns and to use anchors that arise naturally from context; there is no universal safe percentage distribution.
