# Intent Matching in a Content Brief

This reference takes an already-described **search task** and turns it into a content requirement. Its job is briefing: tell the writer what the reader must be able to do after reading the page, what format supports that job, and what evidence is still missing. [Evidence Tier: Tier 3 — Industry observation]

## Briefing method
Write the task first, then map the task to page behavior. For a how-to, the brief should demand prerequisites, sequence, verification, and recovery. For a comparison, it should demand decision criteria, normalized facts, trade-offs, and conditions that change the recommendation. For a definition, it should require a clear concept explanation before deeper context. [Evidence Tier: Tier 3 — Industry observation]

Inspect current SERP formats only to understand expectations. Do not copy headings mechanically. Reserve a dedicated section in the brief for first-party evidence, expert input, or original examples that can make the page more useful than a synthesis of existing results. [Evidence Tier: Tier 3 — Industry observation]

## Concrete example
The query “best project management software for a five-person agency” is not just “project management software information.” The brief states a buyer task, asks for agency-specific selection criteria, requires current integration/pricing evidence, and defines the conversion role. This produces a materially different outline from a generic project-management explainer. [Evidence Tier: Tier 3 — Industry observation]

## Briefing failure modes
A brief is weak when it specifies article length instead of the reader's task, treats all competitor sections as mandatory, adds FAQs without audience evidence, or leaves the evidence owner undefined for claims that can age quickly. [Evidence Tier: Tier 3 — Industry observation]

## Evidence boundary
Intent matching is an observed editorial framework, not a statement of private Google behavior. Undocumented mechanism explanations are **Tier 4 — Hypothesis**. [Evidence Tier: Tier 1 — Officially documented]
