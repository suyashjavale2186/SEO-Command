## Diagnostic sequence
Capture the query exactly as tested, plus locale, device context when available, date, major result types, organic formats, and notable feature modules. Read representative results and record what information is actually being surfaced. Treat the SERP as a current observation that may change, not as a permanent specification. [Evidence Tier: Tier 3 — Industry observation]

## Worked situation
For “best accounting software for freelancers,” the analyst records vendor pages, editorial comparisons, pricing content, and review sites, then identifies the decision criteria common to the comparable results. A later audit can tell whether the result mix changed rather than mistaking the new layout for a content regression. [Evidence Tier: Tier 3 — Industry observation]

## What breaks the tactic
Using an old screenshot; mixing markets; treating an SEO tool’s SERP snapshot as timeless; copying one result instead of the result pattern; or claiming a feature is mandatory because it appeared on one ranking page. [Evidence Tier: Tier 3 — Industry observation]
