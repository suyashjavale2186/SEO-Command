# SERP-driven writing

SERP research should inform the page's intent, information architecture, and answer format, while the finished page adds useful evidence and perspective rather than copying competitors. **[Tier 2–3]**

## Process
- Inspect the current SERP and identify the dominant user task.
- Compare representative leading pages for structure, evidence, format, and missing useful information.
- Choose a content form that matches the observed intent and the business objective.
- Cover necessary concepts naturally; never optimize to a fixed keyword density, fixed word count, title/meta length, or snippet-answer length.
- Add first-party evidence or genuinely useful analysis where available.
- Preserve truthful limitations and scope.

## Evidence discipline
Observed SERP patterns are observations, not proof of causal ranking mechanisms. Claims about internal Google systems must be treated as **Tier 4 — Hypothesis** unless officially documented.

## Generative search
Do not optimize for imaginary “AI citation tricks.” Google documents AI Overviews and AI Mode as part of Search; foundational SEO remains the basis for visibility. **[Tier 1]** https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features
