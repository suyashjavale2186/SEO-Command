# Anti-slop writing ruleset

## Objective
Do not optimize content for an AI-detector score. Optimize for evidence of real thinking, expertise, usefulness, originality, honest uncertainty, and a clear point of view grounded in evidence. **[Tier 4 internal editorial rule]**

## Remove
- Generic scene-setting that delays the answer
- Empty praise and unsupported superlatives
- Repetitive conclusions that merely restate headings
- Corporate filler and vague “in today’s world” framing
- Claims that sound authoritative but have no evidence
- False first-person stories, fake tests, fabricated customer examples, or invented credentials
- Predictable section symmetry when the topic does not naturally require it

## Add
- Specific evidence and sources
- Concrete examples and edge cases
- Explicit trade-offs
- Honest limitations and who the advice does not fit
- First-party observations supplied by the business or expert
- Clear reasoning: why a conclusion follows from the evidence

## Editing principle
Voice injection must come from authentic supplied language and judgment. Never invent a persona or first-hand experience to make content appear human. **[Tier 4 internal editorial rule]**
