# Content Types for Drafting

The `write-content` version of content types answers **“How do we execute the selected format without letting it drift?”** The format has already been chosen; the writer's job is to make the page behave consistently with that format from opening answer through closing action. [Evidence Tier: Tier 3 — Industry observation]

## Drafting rules by format
**Comparison:** organize around buyer criteria, normalize factual differences, expose trade-offs, and state the circumstances that change the recommendation. Do not declare a universal winner when the evidence supports conditional choices. [Evidence Tier: Tier 3 — Industry observation]

**How-to:** put prerequisites near the beginning, make each step operational, distinguish reversible from irreversible actions, and provide a verification method for the reader's own environment. [Evidence Tier: Tier 3 — Industry observation]

**FAQ:** answer real questions that arise from the subject and audience evidence. Keep each answer self-contained enough to be useful, then qualify it where a short answer would mislead. [Evidence Tier: Tier 3 — Industry observation]

**Buying guide:** define the decision criteria before recommending products. Verify current facts and disclose the evidence basis for prices, features, availability, or tests. [Evidence Tier: Tier 1 — Officially documented]

## Concrete scenario
A writer receives a brief for “how to migrate CRM A to CRM B.” The brief chose the how-to format, so the writer does not turn the opening into a generic CRM overview. Instead, the page begins with prerequisites, the migration sequence, checkpoints for data integrity, common failure recovery, and a final verification procedure. [Evidence Tier: Tier 3 — Industry observation]

## Failure modes
Format drift, unsupported first-person testing claims, generic intros that delay the answer, comparison tables populated with stale vendor facts, and “SEO sections” added solely to repeat related phrases are all drafting failures. The cure is to return to the selected task and evidence map. [Evidence Tier: Tier 1 — Officially documented]

## Evidence boundary
Do not use a content-type template as evidence of a hidden Google preference. Such claims are **Tier 4 — Hypothesis** unless formally documented. Recommendations follow the centralized governance contract. [Evidence Tier: Tier 1 — Officially documented]
