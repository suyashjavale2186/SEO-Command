- # Technique 03: Voice & Personality Injection [Evidence Tier: Tier 3 — Industry observation]


- ## What It Is [Evidence Tier: Tier 3 — Industry observation]


- A system for injecting genuine human voice, personality, and specificity into AI-generated content. Removing AI patterns (Technique 02) is necessary but insufficient -- "sterile, voiceless writing is just as obvious as slop" (blader/generic quality-focused rewriting). This technique adds the human qualities that make content engaging, trustworthy, and undetectable. [Evidence Tier: Tier 3 — Industry observation]


- ## Why It Works [Evidence Tier: Tier 3 — Industry observation]


- AI content is detectable primarily because it regresses to the mean of all writing. A specific voice has specific word preferences, sentence rhythms, opinions, and reference domains that break the low-perplexity pattern. Voice injection also directly addresses Google's E-E-A-T "Experience" signal -- content with genuine personality signals a real author with real opinions.
