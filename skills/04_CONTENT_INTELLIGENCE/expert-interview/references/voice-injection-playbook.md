# Voice injection playbook

Use this playbook to preserve authentic expert voice in content produced from interview material. **[Tier 4 internal editorial method]**

## Preserve
- Distinctive phrasing when it clarifies meaning
- Specific judgments and preferences
- Real examples, failures, and trade-offs
- Qualifiers such as “in our environment” or “for this customer segment”
- Evidence attached to the expert's statement

## Transform carefully
Convert spoken language into readable prose without changing meaning. Keep the boundary between first-party experience, expert interpretation, and externally verified fact explicit.

## Never do
- Invent a first-person anecdote
- Invent a number, test, client, credential, or outcome
- Attribute an external fact to the expert
- Turn a tentative view into certainty
- Use first-person language merely as an SEO tactic

## Handoff
Pair the resulting material with the information-gain and E-E-A-T workflows. The downstream writer may quote or paraphrase only what the supplied interview supports.
