## Control points
Start with the story or evidence a journalist, editor, or relevant audience would genuinely care about. Strong campaigns can be built around original datasets, useful benchmarks, expert commentary, unique surveys, or a real business development that is publicly shareable. The link is a potential outcome of editorial interest, not the sole value proposition. [Evidence Tier: Tier 3 — Industry observation]

## Production example
A payments company has anonymized transaction data showing a new pattern in failed card authorizations. The PR plan turns that evidence into a clear industry finding, provides methodology notes, offers an expert to explain the result, and pitches publications whose readers can use the information. ## Failure signatures
Calling routine product announcements “news”; manufacturing data only for backlinks; exaggerating findings; pitching irrelevant publications; or promising a fixed number of placements before editors have evaluated the story. [Evidence Tier: Tier 3 — Industry observation]
