# Partnerships




- # Tactic 14: Strategic Partnerships & Co-Marketing [Evidence Tier: Tier 3 — Industry observation]


- ## What It Is [Evidence Tier: Tier 3 — Industry observation]


- Building genuine business relationships with complementary (non-competing) businesses and leveraging those partnerships for mutual link building. This includes co-authored content, joint webinars, integration partnerships, cross-promotions, podcast collaborations, and co-branded research. Each partner benefits from the other's audience and link equity. [Evidence Tier: Tier 3 — Industry observation]


- ## Why It Works [Evidence Tier: Tier 3 — Industry observation]


- Links arising from genuine partnerships can be legitimate because the relationship itself explains why the link exists, and the partnership may also provide distribution to a relevant audience. Do not assume Google assigns special value simply because a link comes from a partner; evaluate topical relevance, editorial context, and user value. [Evidence Tier: Tier 3 — Industry observation]
