# Governance contract

Before final recommendations, consult `10_GOVERNANCE/evidence-engine` and, when strategic/content implications are material, `10_GOVERNANCE/red-team`.

Every recommendation must carry: Recommendation, Evidence, Confidence, Expected impact, Risk, Effort, Priority, Evidence status.

Never invent first-hand experience. Never turn an internal heuristic into a Google rule. Never use arbitrary numeric SEO targets as requirements. Structured data is a representation layer. Generative-search work must not imply secret optimization tricks.
