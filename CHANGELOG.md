# SuperSEO Elite 1.0.0

## Removed / corrected
- Removed arbitrary keyword-density, title/meta-length, snippet-length, and internal-link-ratio requirements as SEO rules.
- Removed unlabeled claims about Google internal ranking mechanisms; such claims are now Tier 4 hypotheses unless officially documented.
- Removed AI-detector framing from writing quality.
- Removed claims that structured data independently improves rankings or generative-search citations.
- Removed guarantees around links, authority metrics, featured snippets, AI citations, rankings, or traffic.

## Added
- Evidence engine and red-team governance.
- Business context, audience, and conversion models.
- Technical SEO, site graph, internal-link graph, cannibalization, and indexation systems.
- Opportunity scoring and strategic prioritization.
- Generative-search analysis and measurement.
- Content decay detection and content treatment decisions.
- Structured SEO experimentation and site-specific learning.
- Execution-ready technical/content/link actions and roadmap sequencing.

## Preserved and upgraded
- Expert interview question bank and first-party knowledge extraction.
- SERP-driven briefing and writing workflow.
- Anti-slop framework, reframed around evidence, reasoning, usefulness, originality, and truthful voice.
- E-E-A-T decomposition into Experience, Expertise, Authority, Trust.
- Semantic/entity/relationship analysis as an internal diagnostic rather than an asserted Google scoring mechanism.
